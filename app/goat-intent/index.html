Definitions, limitations and source coverage

First-ever: one representative of the earliest qualifying assembly per NCBI species, with no earlier dated Full GenBank genome at any assembly level found in the current/replaced summaries. Upgrade: the first qualifying assembly when an earlier scaffold/contig assembly exists for that species; it may be a new accession, not a revision. Additional: other qualifying assemblies, including same-day companions and species with earlier historical chromosome assemblies. Same-date chromosome assembly ties are resolved by accession for counting only, not as a claim of priority. A lower-level assembly on the same date is part of the first-release cohort: within-day priority is unresolved. An older version of the same accession instead establishes an upgrade.

The historical check is restricted to this research preview. The main Observatory still uses its existing chromosome/complete inventory and counters. Suppressed and Partial assemblies are excluded from historical context. Dates are the public release dates reported by NCBI; historical summaries do not establish the date every assembly first reached a particular quality level. Taxonomic reassignments and unavailable records can leave gaps.

Display precedence: explicit named programme evidence in a linked BioProject title/description or submitter → selected production-centre submitter → research-institution submitter with direct BioProject output below the chosen threshold → other/unresolved. No programme match means “not identified”, not “independent”. Production-centre attribution does not establish who led or funded the work.

Funding is optional and linked papers are incompletely recorded. Institution types are inferred from submitter-name patterns (university/college, institute/academy/laboratory, museum/botanic garden, or company suffix), and can overlap. Grant footprints are lower bounds within the enriched chromosome-scale inventory, not a grant’s total output. Grants are matched by agency plus identifier. We have not yet extracted publication authors/affiliations or verified individual lab leadership. GoaT intent tags are not used to classify the NCBI production effort.

From observed intent to first publication

Time from intent to release is on the horizontal axis; GoaT intent year is on the vertical axis. Each point is one taxon with a definite post-intent first assembly. The box shows the median and middle half of estimated waits; whiskers show the non-outlier range, and faint points show individual taxa.

Waits use the midpoint of each archived observation window; thin horizontal marks show the possible wait range when both bounds are known. The earliest archive is left-censored, so those waits are minimum estimates. “Small groups” is a proxy for taxa with one project tag that is not mapped to a named consortium or institutional programme; it does not verify team size. Mixed or institutional tags stay in All and are omitted from the comparison tabs. Each taxon is counted once.
