Bars show taxon × declaration field × project combinations newly present since the previous snapshot; the line shows declarations present at each checkpoint. From observed intent to first publication

Each point is one taxon with a definite post-intent first assembly, grouped by the year GoaT first observed its direct declaration. The box shows the median and middle half of estimated waits; whiskers show the non-outlier range, and faint points show individual taxa.

Waits use the midpoint of each archived observation window; thin vertical marks show the possible wait range when both bounds are known. The earliest archive is left-censored, so those waits are minimum estimates. “Small groups” is a proxy for taxa with one project tag that is not mapped to a named consortium or institutional programme; it does not verify team size. Mixed or institutional tags stay in All and are omitted from the comparison tabs. Each taxon is counted once.
