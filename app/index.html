Assembly metadata: NCBI Datasets, restricted to GenBank eukaryote assemblies at chromosome or complete level. Annotation status: NCBI RefSeq.

Organism images: Wikimedia/Wikidata with attribution retained when available. Taxa explorer: NCBI Taxonomy, pre-aggregated to phylum and order. Endangered view: IUCN Red List categories via the GBIF-hosted IUCN checklist. Country view: NCBI BioSample geographic location, aggregated to ISO countries. This site tracks public assembly deposition, not raw sequencing-machine output.
