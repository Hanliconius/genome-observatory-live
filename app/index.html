Chromosome labelling

Sex-chromosome labels

Explicit X/Y/Z/W-style labels or explicitly identified other sex-chromosome names in NCBI sequence reports. This measures assembly labelling, not an inferred sex-determination system.

Interpretation

What these metrics mean

Mitochondrial / plastid: an organelle is counted only when NCBI's assembly metadata explicitly associates that organelle genome with the nuclear assembly.

Sex chromosomes: XY labelled requires both X and Y; ZW labelled requires both Z and W. Explicitly identified other sex-chromosome names, and single or non-pair X/Y/Z/W-style labels are shown as Other / partial label.

No label means no explicit sex-chromosome-style chromosome name was found in the sequence report. It does not mean the organism lacks sex chromosomes.

Organism images: Wikimedia/Wikidata with attribution retained when available. Taxa explorer: NCBI Taxonomy, pre-aggregated to phylum and order.
